OUR MIND WHILE WE SLEEP

Have you ever wondered what happens to our mind while we are sleeping?

While our bodies rest, our minds remain surprisingly active, processing emotions, consolidating memories, and navigating vivid imaginary worlds.

Why do we dream and what is a dream?

Every night, our brain goes through different sleep stages that directly influence our mental health and daily focus. On this site, you will discover the science behind dream cycles, learn how to analyze your dreams, and understand how sleep shapes our waking mind.
