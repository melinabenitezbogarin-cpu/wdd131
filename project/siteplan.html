Site Plan - Our Mind While We Sleep

Site Plan Document

Site Name

Site Name: Our Mind While We Sleep

Reason for selection: This name directly reflects the primary focus of the website: investigating subconscious brain activity, explaining sleep phases (such as REM and NREM), and helping visitors discover why we dream and how our minds process information overnight.

Site Purpose

Purpose Statement: The website serves as an educational platform that explains the neurobiology of sleep, dream patterns, and sleep psychology. It aims to offer clear, science-backed answers about dream interpretation, sleep stages, and practical habits for better mental rest.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • What is happening in my brain during REM sleep, and why do I experience vivid or unusual dreams?
  • How do different types of dreams (like lucid dreams or nightmares) occur, and how does sleep affect my memory and brain health?

Color Scheme

  • Primary Background (#111827 - Dark Slate): Used for the main body background.
  • Secondary Background (#1F2937 - Card Gray): Used for section cards, header, and footer containers.
  • Accent Highlight (#10B981 - Neon Mint Green): Used for section headings, logo highlights, and borders.
  • Secondary Accent (#6366F1 - Indigo): Used for interactive links and secondary accents.
  • Primary Text (#F3F4F6 - Off-White): Used for high-contrast, readable text throughout the page.

Typography

  • Roboto Bold (700): Applied to main titles, headings (h1, h2), and primary action buttons to create strong visual emphasis.
  • Roboto Regular (400): Applied to paragraph content, list items, and standard body text for comfortable reading.
  • Roboto Light (300): Applied to secondary metadata, subtitles, footers, and decorative captions.
